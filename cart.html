Before you check out

Orders leave New York within 1 to 2 business days. US standard arrives in 3 to 6 business days and is free over $150, otherwise $8.95. Expedited 2-day is $18.00. Full detail on the shipping policy page.

Thirty days from delivery on unworn items with tags attached. US returns include a prepaid label. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days of inspection. Read the returns and refunds policy.

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal and Apple Pay. Card details are handled by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processor over an encrypted connection and are never stored on our servers. See the terms and conditions.
