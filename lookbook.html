Quiet Rooms

Autumn 2026. Twenty-two pieces, four colours, two days in a Tribeca loft with the windows open.

Shop the collection

The brief was simple: build a collection that works in an apartment on a Sunday and in a meeting on a Tuesday, without changing anything but the shoes. Everything here is in production now. Each look lists the pieces it uses.

01

The coat over nothing much

A heavy melton coat left open over silk. The contrast in weight is the whole idea, and it is why the slip is cut long enough to show below the hem.
