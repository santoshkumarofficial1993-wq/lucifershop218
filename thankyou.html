We would rather make less

Every style is produced in a run of 60 to 200. When a size sells out we do not scramble to reorder a cheaper version of it.

Our patterns are drafted on Mercer Street and sewn by two family-run factories, one in Brooklyn and one outside Porto. We publish what each piece costs us to make, because a $268 dress should be able to explain itself.
