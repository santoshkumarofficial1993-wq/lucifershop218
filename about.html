A small shop that makes its own clothes

Alden & Roe is a women's fashion boutique registered in New York State and trading since 2019. We design, sample and sell from one floor on Mercer Street.

Started because the middle was missing

Rachel Alden spent eleven years as a technical designer for two large American labels. Beatriz Roe ran production for a knitwear mill outside Porto. They kept running into the same gap: a $60 dress that lasts one season, or a $900 dress that is made in the same factory.

Alden & Roe sits between the two. We buy good fabric, cut it carefully, produce in small runs, and publish what each piece costs to make. That is the entire business model. There is no outlet channel, no permanent sale, and no house brand of anything we did not design.

Our standards

Four rules we do not break

Two factories, both named

Sewing happens at Mira Confecções near Porto and at Bushwick Cut & Sew in Brooklyn. Both are audited annually.

Costs published per style

Fabric, labour, trims, freight and our margin appear on every product page. No rounded marketing numbers.

Natural fibres first

Silk, wool, linen, cashmere and organic cotton. Elastane only where a garment genuinely needs recovery, capped at 4%.

No fake urgency

No countdown timers, no invented stock counters, no permanent "final sale". If a size is gone, the page says so.

How we got here

2019

Rachel and Beatriz sample eleven styles in a shared studio on Canal Street and sell them at two weekend markets. Nine styles sell out.

2021

The Mercer Street floor opens as a studio with appointment-only fittings. The first wool overcoat run of 80 units sells through in five weeks.

2023

Coat and outerwear production moves to Brooklyn to cut freight and shorten sampling. Lead time on a new coat drops from 22 weeks to 9.

2025

We start publishing per-style cost breakdowns on every product page after customers kept asking the same question by email.

2026

Twenty-two styles in production, three people on staff, and a returns rate of 11.3% across the last twelve months.
